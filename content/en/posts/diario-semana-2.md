This week I tried to explain recursion to a friend and… I went blank halfway through. **Plot twist**: I didn't understand it as well as I thought.

> [!FEYNMAN]
> My gap: I knew how to *write* recursive functions, but I couldn't explain **why** the answer "comes back down".

I studied again, found the staircase analogy and now I get it. I wrote it up in [Recursion explained with stairs](post.html?p=recursion).

## What I learned

1. Knowing how to do ≠ knowing how to explain.
2. Awkward pauses are clues.
3. ==Analogies stick; definitions fade.==

## How to play

Pick a concept and explain it **like to a 12-year-old**. The detector checks:

- sentences that are too long
- very long words
- jargon (=={pink}in pink==) and very long words (=={lilac}in lilac==)
- whether you use analogies ("it's like…", "imagine…") ✿

```jerga
concepto: what is an algorithm?
```

> [!TIP]
> Words like "obviously", "simply" or "trivial" cost points: they almost always hide a gap.
